🌿 How to Play

Welcome to the Garden Memory Match game! 🌼🧠

  • 🃏 Click on a card to flip it.
  • 🔁 Click another to try and find its match.
  • ✅ If they match, they stay revealed.
  • ❌ If not, they flip back — so try to remember!

Match all pairs to win and become a Garden Genius! 🌻
